FAQ

Answers to Your Questions About Construction.

What is the typical timeline for a construction project?
Timelines vary significantly based on scope and complexity. A small renovation may take a few weeks, while a large commercial build can span 12–24 months. During our initial consultation, we'll provide a realistic timeline tailored to your specific project.
How do I choose the right contractor?
Look for proven track record, proper licensing and insurance, positive client testimonials, and transparent communication. At Brickon, we offer all of this plus a dedicated project manager for every client engagement.
How much does construction cost?
Costs depend on many factors including project size, materials selected, location, and labor. We provide detailed, transparent quotes during our consultation process with no hidden fees or surprises.
What is a contingency budget?
A contingency budget (typically 10–15% of total project cost) is a financial reserve set aside for unexpected costs — such as unforeseen site conditions, design changes, or material price fluctuations.
What materials should I use for my project?
Material selection depends on your budget, aesthetic preferences, climate, and maintenance goals. Our expert team will guide you through options, comparing costs, durability, and environmental impact to find the perfect fit.
Can I make changes during construction?
Yes, changes can typically be accommodated, though they may affect timelines and costs. We encourage finalizing decisions before construction begins, but our flexible team can work with you on reasonable change requests.
