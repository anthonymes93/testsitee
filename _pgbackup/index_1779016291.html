FAQ

Common Questions About Our Electrical Services.

Do you work outside Hamilton?
No — we serve Hamilton homeowners exclusively. By keeping our focus on one city, we can show up faster, build better relationships with local ESA inspectors, and genuinely specialize in Hamilton's housing stock. That focus makes us better at our job.
Do you do commercial electrical work?
We specialize in residential work only. We don't take commercial, industrial, or multi-unit projects — this focus is intentional and lets us be genuinely excellent at what we do for Hamilton homeowners.
Is your work ESA-certified?
Yes. All of our electricians are licensed in Ontario and our work meets ESA (Electrical Safety Authority) standards. We pull permits when required and coordinate inspections on your behalf so you don't have to lift a finger.
How long does a panel upgrade take?
Most residential panel upgrades are completed in a single day. We'll give you a realistic timeline at the quote stage so there are no scheduling surprises — you'll know exactly what to expect before we start.
Do you give free quotes?
Yes, absolutely. We provide free, upfront quotes for all residential jobs. The price we quote is the price you pay — we don't charge by the hour or add surprise fees at invoice time. What we say is what you get.
What areas of Hamilton do you serve?
We cover all Hamilton neighborhoods including the Mountain, Westdale, Dundas, Ancaster, Stoney Creek, Binbrook, and Waterdown. If it's in Hamilton, we come to you — no travel fees, no exceptions.
